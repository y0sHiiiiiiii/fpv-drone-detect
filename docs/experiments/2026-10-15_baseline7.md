# Experiment: 7-class baseline (`baseline7`, yolo11s @ 1280, Halmstad visible)

Date: 2026-10-15 · Phase 6 · Code at training time: `6e5f228` plus the uncommitted 7-class
migration, committed unchanged afterwards as `4d344c0`

The first model trained on all 7 classes. Airplanes and helicopters are now labeled targets
instead of negatives, and background images are crops cut from Halmstad frames. The questions:
how good is the new reference, and are the false tracks on aircraft from the old baseline gone?

## Setup

| | |

### Are the false tracks on aircraft gone?

Every track classified by where most of its boxes sit (IoU ≥ 0.1 with a labeled box or not) and by
its majority class (ad-hoc analysis of the `*_detections.json` files):

| | old baseline (44 old test videos) | `baseline7` (45 new test videos) |
|---|---|---|
| multirotor tracks on aircraft clips | 4 (on the aircraft: `V_AIRPLANE_032` 284 boxes, 2 short ones on helicopters; 1 on background) | **0** |
| bird tracks on an aircraft | 4 (`V_AIRPLANE_008` 117 boxes, `V_HELICOPTER_052` 78, `V_HELICOPTER_055` 106, one of 2 boxes) | 1 (`V_AIRPLANE_015`, 139 boxes) |
| airplane ↔ helicopter tracks | – (classes did not exist) | 3 helicopter tracks on airplanes (`V_AIRPLANE_040/041/048`, 302 boxes) |
| tracks on background in aircraft clips | 2 | 17 (507 boxes `bird`, 75 `airplane`, 1 `helicopter`) |

- **Yes for the old error.** No aircraft clip produces a multirotor track anymore, and the old
  long track on `V_AIRPLANE_032` was exactly that error. (That clip and the other old problem clips are now in the training or validation split,
  so it cannot be re-tested.)
- The remaining aircraft errors are confusions among the aircraft and bird classes. `V_AIRPLANE_015`
  is a small propeller airplane detected as bird. The large airliners in `V_AIRPLANE_040/041/048`
  are sometimes called helicopter.
- **Most of the background tracks are lens dirt.** 7 of the 13 background bird tracks come from
  `V_AIRPLANE_040`: dark specks that stay at the same pixel (standard deviation < 0.5 px over 60
  frames) while the camera follows the airplane, so they are on the lens, not in the sky. They only
  appear in the first ~64 frames of that clip. `V_AIRPLANE_035` (106 boxes) has a tiny speck in the
  sky; whether that is a real distant object is not clear by eye.
- The airplane group's low MOTA (0.30) comes mainly from the 504 wrong-class boxes and the lens
  specks. Halmstad's airplane clips are also cut together from several shorter sequences, and every
  cut starts a new track (`V_AIRPLANE_040`: 5 airplane tracks of ~64 frames each).

## Findings

- The 7-class model is a better reference: no aircraft becomes a multirotor, multirotor P/R on
  video is 0.89 / 0.91, and on the shared videos it beats the old model.
- New error type: airplane ↔ helicopter ↔ bird confusion. 9 airplane test clips are not many, and
  Halmstad's airplanes range from airliners to tiny distant specks.
- Overfitting starts earlier (epoch 21) with more data and classes. Use 25 epochs for further runs
  on this data.
- Halmstad has a few lens-dirt specks that the model detects as birds. That looks like a property
  of the data rather than a model bug, but it inflates the false track count.

## Next steps

2. P2 head with the same data, at 25 epochs.
3. Optional: look through the airplane clips for further lens dirt, and check whether
