**Overfitting.** From about epoch 20 on, the validation box loss rises while the training loss keeps
falling. Validation mAP peaks at epoch 29 and then declines. Neither the falling learning rate nor
`close_mosaic` in the last 10 epochs reversed that. `best.pt` is epoch 29; Ultralytics' final
validation of it reports mAP50 0.881 and mAP50-95 0.429 on val.

Conclusion: Halmstad alone is too small and too homogeneous for 100 epochs. About 30–40 epochs give
the same result in about a third of the time. The next gains have to come from more varied data.

## Analysis of the multirotor false positives

The 745 multirotor false positives at conf 0.25 come from these clip types (greedy IoU ≥ 0.5
matching):

| clip type | images | images with ≥ 1 FP | multirotor FP | bird FP |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DRONE | 916 | 586 | 723 | – |
| BIRD | 412 | 50 | 1 | 72 |
| AIRPLANE | 96 | 20 | 16 | 6 |
| HELICOPTER | 191 | 14 | 5 | 11 |

The airplanes and helicopters in the negative clips are barely an issue. Almost all false
positives are in the drone clips, where every frame contains exactly one drone. Here is how those
723 relate to the drone:

| type | count |
|---|---|
| second box on an already matched drone (IoU ≥ 0.5) | 328 |
| box overlapping the drone but offset (IoU 0.1–0.5) | 381 |
| no overlap with the drone (phantom detection) | 14 |

Their confidence is low: median 0.31, 90th percentile 0.46, maximum 0.64, and only 41 are at
≥ 0.5. **The model finds the drones but often draws two boxes on them.** The NMS with IoU 0.7 does
not merge boxes that overlap less than that.

Method: an ad-hoc analysis of `coco_gt.json` + `coco_predictions.json` from `runs/eval/baseline_test/`
(clip type from the file name, greedy matching by score). It belongs in the phase 6 error-analysis
notebook.

## Problems during the run and their fixes

| problem | cause | fix |
|---|---|---|
| RAM full (~29 GB) in the first attempt | 8 dataloader workers plus a 2× validation loader made 26 Python processes on Windows | `workers: 2` (equally fast, ~12 GB); first attempt discarded (`runs/detect/baseline_aborted_8workers`) |
| epoch 19 took 787 s instead of ~203 s | a test evaluation on the GPU running in parallel with training overflowed the 12 GB VRAM | no GPU eval during training (README); `torch.cuda.empty_cache()` before predict |
| automatic test evaluation crashed (CUDA OOM, 23.7 GiB) | Ultralytics loads a list source into memory as one batch and ignores `batch`; dev runs used ≤ 200 images and never hit it | `evaluate.py` predicts in chunks of `--batch` images, with a regression test (`46ce5cf`) |
| float32 not JSON-serialisable | numpy scalars in `to_coco` | `.tolist()` |

## Limits of this result

- **Domain:** a static ground camera, mostly sky backgrounds, 640×512, three drone models. These
  numbers say nothing about how the model performs on FPV footage, which has motion blur, ground
  backgrounds and a moving camera.
- Halmstad labels only the clip's own class, so unlabeled objects (e.g. distant birds in a drone
  clip) can show up as false positives.
- Only two of the five classes are trained.
